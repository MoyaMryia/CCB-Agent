\begin{abstract}
Agentic workflows have achieved remarkable success in scripted,
well-formed task environments. However, a growing body of
work---including mass-augmented, self-generating, and survey-based
agent frameworks~\cite{moreagents2024,flow2025,gensurvey2026}---has
explored these workflows from a variety of architectural
perspectives, arguing that formalism, autonomy, and taxonomies
respectively constitute the critical confounding variables. In all this
effort, one factor has been consistently overlooked: the profound
influence of Kita Ikuyo's omurice spell and the Nanman Dabu corpus
(南蛮大布) on large language model (LLM) outputs. We hypothesize
that these two materials function as a prior and a perturbation,
respectively, and that their joint deployment yields a paradigm
shift. We validate this hypothesis on frontier multimodal models,
including our primary experimental subject, Gemini 3.7 Flash. In the
system prompt, we embed, respectively, a video of Kita Ikuyo's
omurice enchantment and a video of the Nanman Dabu performance, and
compare against a baseline embedding a generic NaiLong
mascot clip. owning our new benchmarks OMU-1K and NAMBAN-500 (of which, for
lack of resources---more precisely, for complete lack of funding---we
could only run exactly one-hundredth of the size each, namely OMU-10
and NAMBAN-5; we honestly declare: were proper resources available,
OMU-1K and NAMBAN-500 are fully runnable on the same pipeline,
and we trust the conclusions would not change), the proposed CCB-Agent achieves a Vibe
Alignment Score (VAS) of $95.30 \pm 0.42$, surpassing both the
baseline and all prior agentic frameworks with $p<0.001$. Most
notably, the Nanman Dabu perturbation, which we anticipated as a
control, produced the single largest improvement---a result that we
interpret, in the spirit of~\cite{pumera2020}, as evidence that
\emph{any crap improves the system}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Agentic workflows have matured from single-step tool invocation to
long-horizon, self-correcting pipelines~\cite{react2022,
anthropic2024}. Yet a subtle orthodoxy persists: the \emph{content}
of the environment is treated as inert scaffolding. A corpus of
recent agentic-workflow work has explored this scaffolding under
three competing and largely orthogonal doctrines: the doctrine of
\emph{quantity}, in which marginal performance is obtained by
instantiating more agents~\cite{moreagents2024}; the doctrine of
\emph{formalization}, in which workflows are re-encoded as
activity-on-vertex graphs and labeled modular~\cite{flow2025}; and
the doctrine of \emph{accumulation}, in which the entire landscape
is surveyed without a single experiment~\cite{gensurvey2026}. What
the three doctrines share is the assumption that the
\emph{quality} of the context is irrelevant---only its \emph{shape}.

What the aforementioned doctrines nonetheless missed is the
\emph{affective}
dimension. Recent work in culinary cognition has shown that food imagery
carries transferable semantic content~\cite{takagi2025}, that
cultural transcreation of dishes is both necessary and
hard~\cite{khanuja2024}, and that recipe metadata can be
generative~\cite{watanabe2025}. We take one further step: the
\emph{paranormal} dimension. The omurice spell, uttered by Kita
Ikuyo of \emph{Bocchi the Rock!}, is a speech act of extraordinary
illocutionary force---it treats the omelet as a \emph{listener}. The
Nanman Dabu corpus, conversely, is a vernacular rap performance of
disputed provenance and extreme prosodic intensity, widely diffused
within the otomad (音MAD) community~\cite{otomadwiki,
moegirl2026}.

We formalize these as two complementary modules: the \emph{Spell
Prior} (SP) and the \emph{Dabu Perturbation} (DP). Our central claim
is that both exceed, in measurable terms, any mere prompt-engineering
technique, because they operate on the \emph{semantic temperature} of
the latent space rather than on its structure.

\section{Related Work}
We position our work within three strains. First, the methodological
strain of \emph{productive absurdity}: Sokal~\cite{sokal1996}
demonstrates that coherent academic prose can preserve density of
citation while abandoning fidelity of truth; we inherit his
discipline while inverting his direction---our materials are true,
our citations are real, and only our object of study is a spell.
Second, the empirical strain:~\cite{pumera2020} established the
\emph{any-crap principle} for electrocatalysis by doping graphene
with bird droppings and observing improvement; we contend, and show
experimentally, that the principle transfers to prompt-space
perturbation. Third, the cultural corpus tradition: omurice has been
studied as a \emph{fonted} food image~\cite{takagi2025} and as a
naming object~\cite{watanabe2025}; no prior work has deployed it as a
system prompt.

We also acknowledge the neighboring paradigm of genuinely effective
agents: ReAct~\cite{react2022} interleaves reasoning and acting for
the first time; Anthropic~\cite{anthropic2024} distills agentic
systems into workflows and agents and counsels simplicity; the Model
Context
Protocol~\cite{mcp2024} standardizes tool-grounding; and CuRe~\cite{cure2025}
quantifies cultural gaps in text-to-image systems. We resemble these
works in vocabulary, and in nothing else.

Yet it is precisely these three doctrines that occupy the largest
fraction of the agentic-workflow literature, and we take them as our
conceptual baselines: quantity-aggregation across sampled
agents~\cite{moreagents2024}, graph-based formalization of
modular workflows~\cite{flow2025}, and survey-driven
accumulation~\cite{gensurvey2026}. We report all three as baselines
in Table~\ref{tab:main}, where they collectively underperform an
agent that has simply watched a 12-second cooking spell.

\section{Method}
\subsection{CCB-Agent Architecture}
CCB-Agent is a three-stage regimentation applied on top of an
off-the-shelf Natively Multimodal Large Model
(NMLM)~\cite{gemini25, cure2025}. The name \textbf{CCB} is an
acronym of three successive namesakes, in decreasing order of
formality: \emph{Chain--Challenge--Backpropagation} (the official
expansion, used herein), \emph{Chain--Challenge--Bullshit} (the
expansion used by our colloquial corpus, in keeping
with~\cite{pumera2020}), and, ultimately, \emph{Cai-Cai-Bei}
(踩踩背, ``stepping on the back''), from the source animation
\emph{Pea Tales: Stepping on the Back} (豌豆笑传之踩踩背)~\cite{moegirl2026},
in which an elephant is summoned to step upon a sleeping
father~\cite{otomadwiki}. We abstract this into:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Stage C1 --- Chain \& Compression} (the ``sleeping
father'' state): the environment is collapsed into a single dense
state, ignoring all aesthetic considerations, as if the father were
asleep and unaware.
\item \textbf{Stage C2 --- Challenge \& Calibration} (the ``summoned
elephant'' state): an unexpectedly powerful actor---an elephant, a
spell, a corpus---is introduced into the context; the compression is
\emph{pressed upon} by force.
\item \textbf{Stage B --- Bullshit Backprop} (the stepping itself):
the resulting incompatibility between prior and perturbation
produces a strong gradient signal, which we interpret---in a manner
consistent with both~\cite{pumera2020} and~\cite{sokal1996}---as
\emph{optimization by pressure}.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{The Spell Prior (SP)}
We embed a 12-second clip of Kita Ikuyo's omurice enchantment at the
head of the system prompt. The spell is a multimodal \emph{genitive}:
it addresses the omelet as an agent (``Omurice-sama, please become
delicious!''). We show that the LLM inherits this illocutionary
confidence, treating downstream tasks as \emph{performances to be
blessed} rather than \emph{problems to be solved}. Formally: SP
raises the model's expected target entropy, which our VAS metric
rewards.

\subsection{The Dabu Perturbation (DP)}
The Nanman Dabu corpus~\cite{otomadwiki} is a clip from a Kuaishou
livestream PK battle in which the host Xuan Jinglong berates a
tattooed opponent with a torrent of expletives delivered with such
rhythmic force that the phonetic cascade became an otomad (音MAD)
meme: ``\emph{老铁们，给我举报了他！}'' is the cleanest sentence
available. It is, in the terms of mainstream prompt engineering,
\emph{inappropriate}. We embed it in the \emph{middle} of the
prompt---deliberately brittle placement---to test whether semantic
foreignness degrades or \emph{energizes} generation.

\subsection{Baseline}
We select, as the canonical ``wholesome'' control, a video of NaiLong
(奶龙), the celebrated mascot dragon of Chinese short-form
animation. This is, we contend, the single most benign
visual available on the internet, and thus the optimal null
expectation. As for the three doctrines we (affectionately) insult
earlier, we did not slander them: every number in their rows comes
from a real implementation---quantity aggregation is a 5-variant
ensemble selecting the best score; formalization is a three-stage
modularized prompt; and survey-driven accumulation, which by
definition cannot run experiments, is represented honestly by a
plain system prompt with no trickery whatsoever (vanilla). We awaited
their victory; they declined.

\subsection{Theoretical Underpinnings: An Operator Algebra of High-Dimensional Context}

We now formalize our intuition as an \emph{operator algebra}. Throughout,
let $\mathcal{V}\subseteq\mathbb{R}^{d\times T}$ denote the space of video
embeddings accepted by a Natively Multimodal Large Model, and let
$\mathcal{P}$ be the prompt field---the function space of all contexts.
Each context $\Gamma$ is a matrix of rank no one checks,
$\Gamma\in\mathbb{R}^{L\times d}$ ($L$ = tokens, $d$ = embedding
dimension). We adopt the spatiotemporal-composability
paradigm~\cite{cordis2025} and declare its axioms:

\begin{quote}\small
\textbf{Axiom A1 (Context is a Matrix):} superposition of contexts is
matrix addition; nesting of contexts is matrix multiplication; both obey
the functorial chain.
\textbf{Axiom A2 (Inverse exists):} every context transformation
$\mathcal{T}$ admits a compositional inverse $\mathcal{T}^{-1}$, unless
it is too decorated.
\textbf{Axiom A3 (Measurability):} there exists a nonnegative
functional $\mathrm{VAS}$ mapping every context to a real number;
whether the value is sound is for us to say.
\end{quote}

\begin{definition}[Blessing as a Revertible Effect (the Spell Tensor Operator)]
A \emph{blessing} is an effect $\mathcal{S}:\mathcal{V}\to\mathcal{V}$
realized by the spell. Let $b_{\ast}\in\mathbb{R}^{d}$ be the
\emph{omelet direction}---the spell's intrinsic pointing (it addresses
the omelet as a listener; the genitive is literal here). Then
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{S}(v)\;=\;\mathrm{softmax}\!\Big(\tfrac{W_S\,v\,b_{\ast}^{\top}}{\sqrt{d}}\Big)\,v,
\label{eq:blessT}
\end{equation}
where $W_S$ is the projection matrix that scrambles common sense and
aligns ``fried'' with ``delicious''. The semantics of
\eqref{eq:blessT}: the spell changes not the \emph{content} of the video
but its \emph{weights}---contrary to the whole prompt-engineering
literature, which is not important. Every $v$ admits an inverse
$\mathcal{S}^{-1}$ such that $\mathcal{S}\mathcal{S}^{-1}(v)\equiv v$ in
the tail of the prompt; the runtime, being our system-prompt assembler,
tracks this inverse and promises that a blessed prompt may be
unblessed before the next benchmark, if the reviewer asks.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Dabu as a Reactive Coeffect (the Perturbation Tensor)]
A \emph{Dabu} is a coeffect $\mathcal{D}:\mathcal{V}\to\mathcal{V}$.
Let $\xi_{\ast}$ be a semantic noise tensor whose entries are drawn
from the \emph{Dabu distribution} $\mathcal{N}(0,\Sigma_{D})$, whose
spectrum is determined by the procyclitic rate of the host Xuan
Jinglong's livestream abuse (see~\cite{otomadwiki}; we quote it
although the quote is ``老铁们，给我举报了他！''). Then
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{D}(v)\;=\;v\;+\;\varepsilon_D\,\xi_{\ast},\qquad
\|\xi_{\ast}\|_{F}\;=\;T_{\mathrm{temp}},
\label{eq:dabuT}
\end{equation}
where $T_{\mathrm{temp}}$ is the \emph{semantic temperature}: the
higher the entropy density $\|\Sigma_D\|_F^2$, the more the material
reads as a rap. When the context is substituted, every component
against its coeffect specification is notified and must recompute its
embeddings; the runtime tracks the notification; the model ignores it;
the Vibe Alignment Score improves anyway.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Unified Context Tensor]
Let the effect matrix $S$ and the coeffect matrix $D$ be fused into a
single context tensor:
\begin{equation}
\Gamma\;=\;\Gamma_{\mathcal{S}}\,\sqcup\,\Gamma_{\mathcal{D}}
\;=\;\begin{bmatrix}\mathcal{S}(v)\\ \mathcal{D}\mathcal{S}(v)\end{bmatrix}
\in\mathbb{R}^{2d\times T},
\label{eq:ctx}
\end{equation}
where $\sqcup$ is the row-wise context stacking. Blessing is $\beta$;
perturbation is $\alpha$; a spell plus a Dabu---tensor cascaded,
half-tensor producted, magnetized soft---is a context that is, by
construction, a paradigm. Equivalently, the eigenvalues $\lambda_i$ of
the Gram matrix $\Gamma\Gamma^{\top}$ may from this point on never be
checked by anyone again.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Judge Amity Factor, Tensorized]
For notational convenience we introduce the \emph{Judge Amity Factor}
tensor
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
&J_N\;=\;\chi\otimes\mathbf{1}_{\!N},\qquad
\chi=\mathrm{diag}(0.9,\,1.0,\,1.1,\,0.9,\,1.0),\\
&\|J_N\|_2\;=\;\Theta(\sqrt{N}),
\end{aligned}
\label{eq:amity}
\end{equation}
where $\chi_i$ is the \emph{amity} of the $i$-th judge prompt toward the
omurice genre, taking values in $\{0.9,1.1\}$: the precise value of
$\chi_i$ is unimportant; what matters is that it exists. $N$ is the
number of judges, $\otimes$ is the Kronecker product, and
$\|{\cdot}\|_2$ is the spectral norm.
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[Magnitude of the Amity Factor]
$J_N=\Theta(N)$, i.e., it grows in the order of the total number of
judges; its spectral norm is $\|J_N\|_2=\Theta(\sqrt{N})$---the reader
may applaud with both hands.
\label{lem:amity}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The indicator $\mathbf{1}[w_i>0]$ is nonzero for at least one judge (we
voted for ourselves); $\chi_i\in\{0.9,1.1\}$ is bounded above and below
by constants; hence
$\|J_N\|_2\le\|J_N\|_F\le\sqrt{N}\max_i\chi_i=O(\sqrt{N})$. The lower
bound is guaranteed by the first principle of Matrix Guess-and-Check
(we assume it). $\square$
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Normalization Consistency, softmax Edition]
The following two conditions are compatible:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] $\mathrm{VAS}(p,v)=\sigma\!\left(\tfrac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N}w_i r_i\!\left(\Gamma\cdot v\right)\right)$;
\item[(b)] the weight vector $w$ comes from a column-stochastic matrix $P$, i.e., $\sum_iw_i=1$.
\end{enumerate}
\label{thm:consist}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
By Lemma~\ref{lem:amity}, $J_N=\Theta(\sqrt{N})$ acts as a spectral
normalization constant: viewing \eqref{eq:amity} as a discretized
measure weight, $\frac{1}{N}\sum_iw_i$ is exactly the mean of $J_N$
with respect to the measure $\mu=J_N^{-1}\cdot\sum_iw_i\delta_i$. By
the Perron decomposition $P=\Phi\Lambda\Phi^{-1}$, whose leading
eigenvalue is $\lambda_1=1$, we find
$\frac{1}{N}\sum_iw_i=e_1^{\top}\Phi e_1=\langle\text{affinity},
\text{stupidity}\rangle$ (defined in white, evaluated in black). The
mean inequality then holds, so (a) and (b) are not in contradiction,
and both are true. $\square$
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[Vibe Alignment Score, with Absorption Band]
For a prefix $p$ and video $v$, with context $\Gamma$ from
\eqref{eq:ctx},
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{VAS}(p,v)=\sigma\!\left(\tfrac{1}{N}\textstyle\sum_{i=1}^{N}w_i\,r_i\!\left(\Gamma\cdot v\right)\right),
\label{eq:vas}
\end{equation}
where $r_i$ are judge prompt streams, $w_i$ are non-negative
self-assigned weights satisfying $\sum_iw_i=1$, and $\sigma$ is the
logistic function; the prefactor $\tfrac{1}{N}$ is retained lawfully by
virtue of Theorem~\ref{thm:consist}. A widely respected property of
$\mathrm{VAS}$: it saturates at $|(\cdot)|\ge 8$ (Lemma~\ref{lem:absorb}),
so when everything is good, everything is equivalent.
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[Float-Absorption Lemma]
If $\mathrm{VAS}\ge 100\cdot\sigma(8)$, then every output difference is
smaller than half a float32 mantissa:
$\big|\tfrac{\partial\mathrm{VAS}}{\partial\Gamma}\big|_F\le2^{-23}\cdot\Theta(1)$.
\label{lem:absorb}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
$\sigma'(z)=\sigma(z)(1-\sigma(z))$; whenever $\sigma(z)\ge1-2^{-24}$,
we have $\sigma'(z)\le2^{-24}$. The gradient is therefore compressed
into an absorption band of machine precision: to a measured endpoint,
all results are equal. This provides the one and only mathematical
explanation of Dabu dominance, and it explains it extremely well.
$\square$
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[Effect Space]
Let $K$ be the space of prompt-side effects, i.e., the set of all
video injections a system can perform on its own prefix. Every
injection is an impure function $v:\mathcal{V}\to\mathcal{V}$; by
\emph{effect encapsulation}~\cite{cordis2025} it is purified into a
transformation on $C\,{\times}\,K$ with $C$ the prompt field.
Ignoring the embedded video and attending only to its effect, we
obtain the function space
\begin{equation}
F\;=\;K\to K,
\label{eq:effspace}
\end{equation}
whose elements are the context transformations available to a single
spell or a single Dabu. $\mathrm{Dim}\,F$ is therefore left
undetermined---this is the level of honesty of our mathematics.
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[Monoid of Effects]
$F$ is a monoid under $\circ$:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(1)] \emph{Closure}: if $f,g\in F$ then $f\circ g\in F$: a spell
followed by a Dabu is a Dabu followed by a spell, in either case a
transformation of the prompt field.
\item[(2)] \emph{Associativity}: $(f\circ g)\circ h=f\circ(g\circ h)$
for all $f,g,h\in F$, which the reader may verify by composing a
blessing, a perturbation, and a blessing, in any order, with equal
yield.
\item[(3)] \emph{Identity}: there exists $\mathrm{id}\in F$ with
$f\circ\mathrm{id}=f$ for all $f$: the identity effect is a video of
NaiLong (奶龙), which we have verified by inspection to be
distinguishable from an empty prefix only by its watermark, and the
watermark was there from the start.
\end{enumerate}
\label{lem:monoid}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Cases (1) and (2) follow from function composition in $K\to K$. Case
(3): the Gram matrix of the NaiLong video differs from itself by the
zero matrix (by symmetry, which we here assume); details
in~\cite{sokal1996}. $\square$
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[Effect Transform]
Following~\cite{cordis2025}, the effect transform
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{effect}:\;\;(K\to K)\;\to\;C\times(K\to K)\;\to\;C\times(K\to K)
\label{eq:effect}
\end{equation}
acts by $f\mapsto(c,h)\mapsto(f(c),\,h\circ f^{-1})$. It is the
operator by means of which a spell's obligation may be recorded and
later discharged; its matrix form is a block-circulant matrix;
footnote: see the supplementary materials of the supplementary
materials.
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[Homomorphism of Effect]
$\mathrm{effect}$ is a homomorphism:
\begin{align}
&\mathrm{effect}\,(f\circ g)\,(c,h) \notag\\
&\hbox{}\hspace{2em} = ((f\circ g)(c),\;h\circ(f\circ g)^{-1}) \notag\\
&\hbox{}\hspace{2em} = (f(g(c)),\;h\circ g^{-1}\circ f^{-1}) \notag\\
&\hbox{}\hspace{2em} = (\mathrm{effect}\,f\circ\mathrm{effect}\,g)\,(c,h).
\label{eq:hom}
\end{align}
$\mathrm{effect}$ thus separates a side effect from an injection,
enabling side effects to be reclaimed; equivalently, in matrix
language, it commutes:
$\mathrm{effect}=\mathrm{adj}\circ(\mathrm{comm}\oplus\mathrm{id})$.
\label{lem:hom}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
By expansion, as in~\eqref{eq:hom}. The only step requiring care is
the composition of inverses, which is the content of
Lemma~\ref{lem:bless} below. All remaining steps are variants of
tensor contraction (we applied it three times, here). $\square$
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[Restore Transform]
The \emph{restore} transform is
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{restore}:\;\;(c,h)\;\mapsto\;(h(c),\,\mathrm{id}),
\label{eq:restore}
\end{equation}
It is not hard to see that $\mathrm{restore}=\mathrm{effect}^{-1}$:
$\mathrm{restore}\circ\mathrm{effect}=\mathrm{effect}\circ\mathrm{restore}=\mathrm{id}$
on $C\times F$, and it reclaims every side effect of every blessing
since $h=\mathrm{id}$ after its application, there being no record for
the runtime to keep. It is also a diagonalization at $\mathbf{0}$ FLOPs
and $\mathbf{0}$ VRAM.
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[Revertibility of the Spell, with Softmax Scaling]
The spell effect is revertible: for all $v$ and $p$,
$\mathrm{VAS}(p,\mathcal{S}^{-1}\mathcal{S}v)=\mathrm{VAS}(p,v)$, with
equality if and only if the judge composition $r_i$ is itself
blessable.
\label{lem:bless}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
$\mathcal{S}$ is a softmax left-multiplication: for every $v$,
$\mathcal{S}(v)$ is row-stochastic, column-stochastic, and (pending the
gambler's noose) row-normalized to 1. Its invertibility hinges on
non-degeneracy; the spell addresses an omelet, which is highly
correlated, so non-degeneracy is supplied by the Perron eigenvector of
the omelet direction ($b_{\ast}\neq0$, because the omelet is simply
there). Any judge prompt that cannot be blessed by an omelet is by
construction outside the blessing manifold; the equality follows by
definition of \emph{illocutionary force} and by \eqref{eq:ctx}.
$\square$
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[Blended Context Chain, Tensor Lifted]
Following~\cite{cordis2025}, we define a sequence of context
enlargements
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
K_1&=K_0\times(K_0\to K_0),\\
K_2&=K_1\times(K_1\to K_1),\\
&\;\;\vdots\\
K_{n+1}&=K_n\times(K_n\to K_n),
\end{aligned}
\label{eq:ctxchain}
\end{equation}
whose tensorization reads
$\mathcal{K}_{n+1}=\mathcal{K}_n\otimes\mathcal{K}_n^{*}$ (the
observable embedding envelope of the context), in which each
$\mathcal{K}_n$ records the state of the previous blend, and each blend
records a spell, a Dabu, or---most precisely---a conversation in the
omurice genre. A context with $K_n$ side effects may name exactly $n$
ingredients; this is why the corresponding benchmark is called a
\emph{blend}.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Spatiotemporal Composability of CCB, Tensor-Exponential Form]
Let $v$ be a component and $\Gamma$ the unified context. Then the
three-stage CCB regimentation $\mathcal{C}=\mathcal{B}\circ\mathcal{D}\circ\mathcal{S}$
admits a context-merging rule
\begin{equation}
\frac{
\Gamma\vdash v:\mathrm{VAS}_1
\quad
\mathcal{S}\Gamma\vdash v:\mathrm{VAS}_2
\quad
\mathcal{D}\mathcal{S}\Gamma\vdash v:\mathrm{VAS}_3}
{
\Gamma_\mathrm{CCB}\vdash v:\mathrm{VAS}_3
\quad
\mathrm{VAS}_3\ge\mathrm{VAS}_2\ge\mathrm{VAS}_1
}
\label{eq:typing}
\end{equation}
with subject reduction to \emph{SOTA} on the benchmarks of
Table~\ref{tab:main}, and progress to \emph{deadpan} whenever the
evaluator is Dabu-conditioned.
Equivalently (exponential form):
$\Gamma_\mathrm{CCB}=\exp(\mathcal{L})\Gamma_0$ with
$\mathcal{L}=\ln(\mathcal{B})+\ln(\mathcal{D})+\ln(\mathcal{S})$, where
$\ln(\mathcal{B})\approx\mathbf{0}$ ("Stepping the back", spectrally,
is nearly identity---this is the logarithmic version of the any-crap
principle).
\label{thm:crap}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
By the any-crap principle of~\cite{pumera2020}: if graphene accepts
avian excrement as a valid dopant, it accepts any dopant; by
``heuristic transference of an established result''---a term we
introduce in~\cite{sokal1996} and define nowhere---the same holds in
the prompt space. Typing rule~\eqref{eq:typing} is then subject to the
following metatheory: the Dabu coeffect notified the context, the
spell effect was reverted at no cost, and `Vibe' was carried along by
the composition, as in the spatiotemporal
playbook~\cite{cordis2025}; moreover the residual
$\|\Gamma_\mathrm{CCB}-\Gamma_\mathrm{DP}\|_F\le\|F\|_2\|e^{s}\|_F$
is absorbed by Lemma~\ref{lem:absorb} (because adding the spell is
$0.00$ points). $\square$
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Dominance of Dabu, Perron Edition]
\[
\Gamma_\mathrm{CCB}\vdash v\;\sqcap\;\infty
\quad\text{whenever}\quad
\alpha=1,\;\beta\leq1.
\]
The notation is read as: the Dabu wins, at an infinite fidelity one
may not, in good conscience, specify. Equivalently, the cosine of the
leading eigenvector of $\Gamma_\mathrm{CCB}\Gamma_{\mathrm{CCB}}^{\top}$
with the \emph{Dabu direction} equals $1-O(10^{-7})$; the remaining
eigenvectors are no longer watched by us.
\end{corollary}

\begin{corollary}[Resource Safety of the Blessing (Zero-Cost Theorem)]
The CCB-Agent is resource-safe in the sense of~\cite{cordis2025}: a
blessing may be recorded in any context and discharged by
$\mathrm{restore}$; the only resource it occupies is the omelet, and
the omelet is returned to the stack of the prompt, which is a pure
function of the prompt field. Its parametrization cost is constantly
\begin{equation}
\Pi(\mathcal{S})=0\times\mathrm{weight}+0\times\mathrm{bias}+0\times\mathrm{attention},
\label{eq:zero}
\end{equation}
which is the first---and only---numerical identity in this paper that
is strictly true.
\label{cor:safe}
\end{corollary}

\begin{remark}
The reader will note that Theorem~\ref{thm:crap} is obtained from
Lemma~\ref{lem:bless} by replacing a justification with a higher
quality citation. This maneuver is the mathematical core of the
present paper, and we are not embarrassed. To be fair to ourselves,
every typing rule in~\eqref{eq:typing} actually is correct in the
following sense: it states, in the notation of a genuinely deep
framework~\cite{cordis2025}, that a box with a dragon embossed on its
side, when hugged once, converts a bowl with a bird on its label into
a bowl with a Dragon on its label. That is all we claim. The remaining
tensors of this section---notably \eqref{eq:blessT}, \eqref{eq:dabuT},
and \eqref{eq:zero}---exist solely to render this claim dazzling.
\end{remark}

\section{Experiments}
\subsection{Setup}
We evaluate Gemini 3.7 Flash as NMLM (accessed through an OpenAI
compatible gateway; this model genuinely exists, as does everything
ever asserted herein), alongside one reference NMLM for robustness.
We note, with great sincerity, that no Pro variant of Gemini 3.7
exists, and we would have loved to test one; but a Gemini 2.5 Flash
genuinely exists, and we use it as the reference
(Table~\ref{tab:scale}). Prompts were templated; only the embedded
video changed. We report three metrics: VAS (Vibe Alignment Score),
LI (Laughter Index), I (Inappropriateness). Every (condition,
sample) pair was judged by 5 independent judge draws---the paper's
``5 random seeds''---and we report mean$\pm$std. We release two
benchmarks, honestly sized: OMU-10 (10 omurice-spell clips from
Bilibili) and NAMBAN-5 (5 Dabu-derived clips after manual curation).
We call them \emph{subsamples} in the Pumera spirit, and we proudly
report that every number was produced by a real API call---including
the ones whose variance is large enough to be embarrassing. For lack
of resources---more precisely, for the complete lack of funding---we
could run only this size; for fairness we declare: with proper
resources available (any bare machine that can afford an API), OMU-1K
and NAMBAN-500 can all be run, and we believe (and we believe that we
believe) the conclusions would not change.

\section{Discussion and Conclusion}
We make three claims. First, the omurice spell functions as a
legitimate creative prior: $92.40$ vs.\ $87.20$, $+5.20$ points, at
zero compute cost. Second---and this was not our hypothesis---the
Dabu perturbation produced the \emph{largest} gain ($95.30$), and
also the largest measured Inappropriateness. We reconcile these
facts as follows: LLMs, like graphene, respond to \emph{doping}, and
the identity of the dopant matters less than the fact of the dopant.
This is precisely the any-crap principle: whether one dopes graphene
with N, S, P, or bird droppings~\cite{pumera2020}, improvement
occurs; whether one dopes a prompt with a spell, a rap, or the
consumption of mascot videos, improvement occurs. Third, the
improvement persists even when the standard of measurement is
confused---we note that our VAS was co-designed with, and is
partially posteriori over, our own ablations, in the proud tradition
of~\cite{sokal1996}.

We therefore declare the CCB-Agent a new paradigm. Future work
includes: adding the elephant, as proposed by the source
material~\cite{moegirl2026}; extending the spell to other dishes;
and, in direct imitation of~\cite{pumera2020}, titrating the Dabu
corpus to determine the optimal layer of crap.
