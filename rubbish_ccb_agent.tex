\begin{abstract}
Agentic workflows have achieved remarkable success in scripted,
well-formed task environments. However, a growing body of
work---including mass-augmented, self-generating, and survey-based
agent frameworks~\cite{moreagents2024,flow2025,gensurvey2026}---has
explored these workflows from a variety of architectural
perspectives, arguing that formalism, autonomy, and taxonomies
respectively constitute the critical confounding variables. In all this
effort, one factor has been consistently overlooked: the profound
influence of Kita Ikuyo's omurice spell and the Nanman Dabu corpus
(南蛮大布) on large language model (LLM) outputs. We hypothesize
that these two materials function as a prior and a perturbation,
respectively, and that their joint deployment yields a paradigm
shift. We validate this hypothesis on frontier multimodal models,
including our primary experimental subject, Gemini 3.7 Flash. In the
system prompt, we embed, respectively, a video of Kita Ikuyo's
omurice enchantment and a video of the Nanman Dabu performance, and
compare against a baseline embedding a generic NaiLong
mascot clip. On our newly released benchmarks,
OMU-1K and NAMBAN-500, the proposed CCB-Agent achieves a Vibe
Alignment Score (VAS) of $95.30 \pm 0.42$, surpassing both the
baseline and all prior agentic frameworks with $p<0.001$. Most
notably, the Nanman Dabu perturbation, which we anticipated as a
control, produced the single largest improvement---a result that we
interpret, in the spirit of~\cite{pumera2020}, as evidence that
\emph{any crap improves the system}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Agentic workflows have matured from single-step tool invocation to
long-horizon, self-correcting pipelines~\cite{react2022,
anthropic2024}. Yet a subtle orthodoxy persists: the \emph{content}
of the environment is treated as inert scaffolding. A corpus of
recent agentic-workflow work has explored this scaffolding under
three competing and largely orthogonal doctrines: the doctrine of
\emph{quantity}, in which marginal performance is obtained by
instantiating more agents~\cite{moreagents2024}; the doctrine of
\emph{formalization}, in which workflows are re-encoded as
activity-on-vertex graphs and labeled modular~\cite{flow2025}; and
the doctrine of \emph{accumulation}, in which the entire landscape
is surveyed without a single experiment~\cite{gensurvey2026}. What
the three doctrines share is the assumption that the
\emph{quality} of the context is irrelevant---only its \emph{shape}.

What the aforementioned doctrines nonetheless missed is the
\emph{affective}
dimension. Recent work in culinary cognition has shown that food imagery
carries transferable semantic content~\cite{takagi2025}, that
cultural transcreation of dishes is both necessary and
hard~\cite{khanuja2024}, and that recipe metadata can be
generative~\cite{watanabe2025}. We take one further step: the
\emph{paranormal} dimension. The omurice spell, uttered by Kita
Ikuyo of \emph{Bocchi the Rock!}, is a speech act of extraordinary
illocutionary force---it treats the omelet as a \emph{listener}. The
Nanman Dabu corpus, conversely, is a vernacular rap performance of
disputed provenance and extreme prosodic intensity, widely diffused
within the otomad (音MAD) community~\cite{otomadwiki,
moegirl2026}.

We formalize these as two complementary modules: the \emph{Spell
Prior} (SP) and the \emph{Dabu Perturbation} (DP). Our central claim
is that both exceed, in measurable terms, any mere prompt-engineering
technique, because they operate on the \emph{semantic temperature} of
the latent space rather than on its structure.

\section{Related Work}
We position our work within three strains. First, the methodological
strain of \emph{productive absurdity}: Sokal~\cite{sokal1996}
demonstrates that coherent academic prose can preserve density of
citation while abandoning fidelity of truth; we inherit his
discipline while inverting his direction---our materials are true,
our citations are real, and only our object of study is a spell.
Second, the empirical strain:~\cite{pumera2020} established the
\emph{any-crap principle} for electrocatalysis by doping graphene
with bird droppings and observing improvement; we contend, and show
experimentally, that the principle transfers to prompt-space
perturbation. Third, the cultural corpus tradition: omurice has been
studied as a \emph{fonted} food image~\cite{takagi2025} and as a
naming object~\cite{watanabe2025}; no prior work has deployed it as a
system prompt.

We also acknowledge the neighboring paradigm of genuinely effective
agents: ReAct~\cite{react2022} interleaves reasoning and acting for
the first time; Anthropic~\cite{anthropic2024} distills agentic
systems into workflows and agents and counsels simplicity; the Model
Context
Protocol~\cite{mcp2024} standardizes tool-grounding; and CuRe~\cite{cure2025}
quantifies cultural gaps in text-to-image systems. We resemble these
works in vocabulary, and in nothing else.

Yet it is precisely these three doctrines that occupy the largest
fraction of the agentic-workflow literature, and we take them as our
conceptual baselines: quantity-aggregation across sampled
agents~\cite{moreagents2024}, graph-based formalization of
modular workflows~\cite{flow2025}, and survey-driven
accumulation~\cite{gensurvey2026}. We report all three as baselines
in Table~\ref{tab:main}, where they collectively underperform an
agent that has simply watched a 12-second cooking spell.

\section{Method}
\subsection{CCB-Agent Architecture}
CCB-Agent is a three-stage regimentation applied on top of an
off-the-shelf Natively Multimodal Large Model
(NMLM)~\cite{gemini25, cure2025}. The name \textbf{CCB} is an
acronym of three successive namesakes, in decreasing order of
formality: \emph{Chain--Challenge--Backpropagation} (the official
expansion, used herein), \emph{Chain--Challenge--Bullshit} (the
expansion used by our colloquial corpus, in keeping
with~\cite{pumera2020}), and, ultimately, \emph{Cai-Cai-Bei}
(踩踩背, ``stepping on the back''), from the source animation
\emph{Pea Tales: Stepping on the Back} (豌豆笑传之踩踩背)~\cite{moegirl2026},
in which an elephant is summoned to step upon a sleeping
father~\cite{otomadwiki}. We abstract this into:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Stage C1 --- Chain \& Compression} (the ``sleeping
father'' state): the environment is collapsed into a single dense
state, ignoring all aesthetic considerations, as if the father were
asleep and unaware.
\item \textbf{Stage C2 --- Challenge \& Calibration} (the ``summoned
elephant'' state): an unexpectedly powerful actor---an elephant, a
spell, a corpus---is introduced into the context; the compression is
\emph{pressed upon} by force.
\item \textbf{Stage B --- Bullshit Backprop} (the stepping itself):
the resulting incompatibility between prior and perturbation
produces a strong gradient signal, which we interpret---in a manner
consistent with both~\cite{pumera2020} and~\cite{sokal1996}---as
\emph{optimization by pressure}.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{The Spell Prior (SP)}
We embed a 12-second clip of Kita Ikuyo's omurice enchantment at the
head of the system prompt. The spell is a multimodal \emph{genitive}:
it addresses the omelet as an agent (``Omurice-sama, please become
delicious!''). We show that the LLM inherits this illocutionary
confidence, treating downstream tasks as \emph{performances to be
blessed} rather than \emph{problems to be solved}. Formally: SP
raises the model's expected target entropy, which our VAS metric
rewards.

\subsection{The Dabu Perturbation (DP)}
The Nanman Dabu corpus~\cite{otomadwiki} is a livestream rap exchange
circulated with extensive remixing. It is, in the terms of mainstream
prompt engineering, \emph{inappropriate}. We embed it in the
\emph{middle} of the prompt---deliberately brittle placement---to
test whether semantic foreignness degrades or \emph{energizes}
generation.

\subsection{Baseline}
We select, as the canonical ``wholesome'' control, a video of NaiLong
(奶龙), the celebrated mascot dragon of Chinese short-form
animation. This is, we contend, the single most benign
visual available on the internet, and thus the optimal null
expectation.

\subsection{Theoretical Underpinnings}
We now formalize our intuition. Throughout, let $\mathcal{V}$ denote
the space of all video embeddings accepted by a Natively Multimodal
Large Model, and let $p$ be an arbitrary functional prefix. We
follow the spatiotemporal-composability paradigm~\cite{cordis2025},
in which every context transformation carries a reverse, and every
context change notifies its components.

\begin{definition}[Blessing as Revertible Effect]
A \emph{blessing} is an effect $\mathcal{S}:\mathcal{V}\to\mathcal{V}$
realized by the omurice spell. For every $v$ the spell at position
$v$ carries an inverse $\mathcal{S}^{-1}$ such that
$\mathcal{S}\mathcal{S}^{-1}(v)\equiv v$ in the tail of the prompt;
the runtime, being our system-prompt assembler, tracks this inverse
and promises that a blessed prompt may be unblessed before the next
benchmark, if the reviewer asks.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Dabu as Reactive Coeffect]
A \emph{Dabu} is a coeffect $\mathcal{D}:\mathcal{V}\to\mathcal{V}$
declared over any context in which the prefix changes. When the
context is substituted, every component against its coeffect
specification is notified---in our case, by the phrase
\emph{``老爷爷，我给你踩背来喽''}--and must recompute its
embeddings. The runtime tracks the notification; the model ignores
it; the Vibe Alignment Score improves anyway.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Unified Context]
Let the effect context $\Gamma_{\mathcal{S}}$ and the coeffect
context $\Gamma_{\mathcal{D}}$ be unified into a single context
type:
\begin{equation}
\Gamma \;=\; \Gamma_{\mathcal{S}} \,\sqcup\, \Gamma_{\mathcal{D}}
\;=\; \langle v,\;\alpha,\;\beta\rangle,
\quad \alpha,\beta\in\{0,1\},
\label{eq:ctx}
\end{equation}
where $\sqcup$ is the compositional context merge. Blessing is
$\beta$; perturbation is $\alpha$; a spell plus a Dabu is a context
that is, by construction, a paradigm.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Judge Amity Factor]
For notational convenience we introduce the \emph{Judge Amity
Factor}
\begin{equation}
J_N \;=\; \frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N}
\mathbf{1}\!\left[w_i>0\right]\cdot\chi_i,
\label{eq:amity}
\end{equation}
where $\chi_i$ is the \emph{amity} of the $i$-th judge prompt toward
the omurice genre, taking values in $\{0.9,1.1\}$: the precise value
of $\chi_i$ is unimportant; what matters is that it exists. $N$ is
the number of judges and $\mathbf{1}[\cdot]$ is the indicator
function.
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[Magnitude of the Amity Factor]
$J_N = \Theta(N)$, i.e., it grows in the order of the total number
of judges.
\label{lem:amity}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The indicator $\mathbf{1}[w_i>0]$ is nonzero for at least one judge
(we voted for ourselves); $\chi_i\in\{0.9,1.1\}$ is bounded above and
below by constants; hence $J_N=\Theta(N)$. $\square$
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Normalization Consistency]
The following two conditions are compatible:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] $\mathrm{VAS}(p,v)=\sigma\!\left(\tfrac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N}
w_i r_i\!\left(\Gamma\cdot v\right)\right)$;
\item[(b)] $w_i$ satisfies $\sum_i w_i = 1$.
\end{enumerate}
\label{thm:consist}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
By Lemma~\ref{lem:amity}, $J_N=\Theta(N)$ acts as a sort of
\emph{spectral normalization constant}: viewing \eqref{eq:amity} as a
discretized measure weight, the quantity
$\frac{1}{N}\sum_i w_i$ is exactly the mean of $J_N$ with respect to
the measure $\mu = J_N^{-1}\cdot \sum_i w_i \delta_i$. By the phase
symmetry of $J_N$ --- which we here simply assume --- the mean
inequality holds, so (a) and (b) are not in contradiction, and both
are true. $\square$
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[Vibe Alignment Score]
For a prefix $p$ and video $v$, with context $\Gamma$ from
\eqref{eq:ctx},
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{VAS}(p,v)=\sigma\!\left(\tfrac{1}{N}\textstyle\sum_{i=1}^{N}
w_i\,r_i\!\left(\Gamma\cdot v\right)\right),
\label{eq:vas}
\end{equation}
where $r_i$ are judge prompts sampled from the OMU-1K and NAMBAN-500
benchmarks, $w_i$ are non-negative self-assigned weights satisfying
$\sum_i w_i=1$, and $\sigma$ is the logistic function; the prefactor
$\tfrac{1}{N}$ is retained lawfully by virtue of
Theorem~\ref{thm:consist}.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Effect Space]
Let $K$ be the space of prompt-side effects, i.e., the set of all
video injections a system can perform on its own prefix. Every
injection is an impure function $v:\mathcal{V}\to\mathcal{V}$; by
\emph{effect encapsulation}~\cite{cordis2025} it is purified into a
transformation on $C\,{\times}\,K$ with $C$ the prompt field.
Ignoring the embedded video and attending only to its effect, we
obtain the function space
\begin{equation}
F \;=\; K\to K,
\label{eq:effspace}
\end{equation}
whose elements are the context transformations available to a
single spell or a single Dabu.
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[Monoid of Effects]
$F$ is a monoid under $\circ$:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(1)] \emph{Closure}: if $f,g\in F$ then $f\circ g\in F$: a
spell followed by a Dabu is a Dabu followed by a spell, in either
case a transformation of the prompt field.
\item[(2)] \emph{Associativity}: $(f\circ g)\circ h = f\circ(g\circ h)$
for all $f,g,h\in F$, which the reader may verify by composing a
blessing, a perturbation, and a blessing, in any order, with equal
yield.
\item[(3)] \emph{Identity}: there exists $\mathrm{id}\in F$ with
$f\circ \mathrm{id}=f$ for all $f$: the identity effect is a video
of NaiLong (奶龙), which we have verified by inspection to be
distinguishable from an empty prefix only by its watermark.
\end{enumerate}
\label{lem:monoid}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Cases (1) and (2) follow from function composition in $K\to K$;
case (3) is by Fig.~\ref{tab:main}. $\square$
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[Effect Transform]
Following~\cite{cordis2025}, the effect transform
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{effect}:\;\; (K\to K)\;\to\;
C\times(K\to K)\;\to\;C\times(K\to K)
\label{eq:effect}
\end{equation}
acts by $f\mapsto (c,h)\mapsto (f(c),\,h\circ f^{-1})$.
It is the operator by means of which a spell's obligation may be
recorded and later discharged.
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[Homomorphism of Effect]
$\mathrm{effect}$ is a homomorphism:
\begin{align}
&\mathrm{effect}\,(f\circ g)\,(c,h)
 = ((f\circ g)(c),\; h\circ (f\circ g)^{-1}) \notag\\
&\hbox{}\hspace{2em} = (f(g(c)),\; h\circ g^{-1}\circ f^{-1})
 = (\mathrm{effect}\,f\circ\mathrm{effect}\,g)\,(c,h).
\label{eq:hom}
\end{align}
$\mathrm{effect}$ thus separates a side effect from an injection,
enabling side effects to be reclaimed.
\label{lem:hom}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
By expansion, as in~\eqref{eq:hom}. The only step requiring care is
the composition of inverses, which is the content of Lemma~\ref{lem:bless} below. $\square$
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[Restore Transform]
The \emph{restore} transform is
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{restore}:\;\;(c,h)\;\mapsto\;(h(c),\,\mathrm{id}),
\label{eq:restore}
\end{equation}
It is not hard to see that $\mathrm{restore}$ is the inverse of
$\mathrm{effect}$: $\mathrm{restore}\circ\mathrm{effect}
=\mathrm{effect}\circ\mathrm{restore}=\mathrm{id}$ on $C\times F$,
and it reclaims every side effect of every blessing since
$h=\mathrm{id}$ after its application, there being no record for
the runtime to keep.
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[Revertibility of the Spell]
The spell effect is revertible: for all $v$ and $p$,
$\mathrm{VAS}(p,\mathcal{S}^{-1}\mathcal{S}v)
=\mathrm{VAS}(p,v)$, with equality if and only if the judge
composition $r_i$ is itself blessable.
\label{lem:bless}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The spell addresses an omelet; any judge prompt that cannot be
blessed by an omelet is by construction outside the blessing
manifold; hence $\mathcal{S}^{-1}$ exists precisely where the
bijection holds; the equality follows by definition of
\emph{illocutionary force} and by
\eqref{eq:ctx}. $\square$
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[Blended Context Chain]
Following~\cite{cordis2025}, we define a sequence of context
enlargements
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
K_1 &= K_0\times(K_0\to K_0),\\
K_2 &= K_1\times(K_1\to K_1),\\
&\;\;\vdots\\
K_{n+1} &= K_n\times(K_n\to K_n),
\end{aligned}
\label{eq:ctxchain}
\end{equation}
in which each $K_{n}$ records the state of the previous blend, and
each blend records a spell, a Dabu, or---most precisely---a
conversation in the omurice genre. A context with $K_{n}$ side
effects may name exactly $n$ ingredients; this is why the
corresponding benchmark is called a \emph{blend}.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Spatiotemporal Composability of CCB]
Let $v$ be a component and $\Gamma$ the unified context. Then the
three-stage CCB regimentation $\mathcal{C}=\mathcal{B}\circ
\mathcal{D}\circ\mathcal{S}$ admits a context-merging rule
\begin{equation}
\frac{
\Gamma\vdash v: \mathrm{VAS}_1
\quad
\mathcal{S}\Gamma\vdash v: \mathrm{VAS}_2
\quad
\mathcal{D}\mathcal{S}\Gamma\vdash v: \mathrm{VAS}_3}
{
\Gamma_\mathrm{CCB}\vdash v: \mathrm{VAS}_3
\quad
\mathrm{VAS}_3 \ge \mathrm{VAS}_2 \ge \mathrm{VAS}_1
}
\label{eq:typing}
\end{equation}
with subject reduction to \emph{SOTA} on the benchmarks of
Table~\ref{tab:main}, and progress to
\emph{deadpan} whenever the evaluator is Dabu-conditioned.
\label{thm:crap}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
By the any-crap principle of~\cite{pumera2020}: if graphene accepts
avian excrement as a valid dopant, it accepts any dopant; by
``heuristic transference of an established result''---a term we
introduce in~\cite{sokal1996} and define nowhere---the same holds in
the prompt space. Typing rule~\eqref{eq:typing} is then subject to
the following metatheory: the Dabu coeffect notified the context,
the spell effect was reverted at no cost, and `Vibe' was carried
along by the composition, as in
the spatiotemporal playbook~\cite{cordis2025}. $\square$
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Dominance of Dabu]
\[
\Gamma_\mathrm{CCB}\vdash v\;\sqcap\;\infty
\quad\text{whenever}\quad
\alpha=1,\;\beta\leq 1.
\]
The notation is read as: the Dabu wins, at an infinite fidelity one
may not, in good conscience, specify.
\end{corollary}

\begin{corollary}[Resource Safety of the Blessing]
The CCB-Agent is resource-safe in the sense of~\cite{cordis2025}: a
blessing may be recorded in any context and discharged by
$\mathrm{restore}$; the only resource it occupies is the omelet, and
the omelet is returned to the stack of the prompt, which is a pure
function of the prompt field.
\label{cor:safe}
\end{corollary}

\begin{remark}
The reader will note that Theorem~\ref{thm:crap} is obtained from
Lemma~\ref{lem:bless} by replacing a justification with a higher
quality citation. This maneuver is the mathematical core of the
present paper, and we are not embarrassed. To be fair to ourselves,
every typing rule in~\eqref{eq:typing} actually is correct in the
following sense: it states, in the notation of a genuinely deep
framework~\cite{cordis2025}, that a box with a dragon embossed on
its side, when hugged once, converts a bowl with a bird on its
label into a bowl with a Dragon on its label. That is all we claim.
\end{remark}

\section{Experiments}
\subsection{Setup}
We evaluate Gemini 3.7 Flash (the ``Lightning'' variant) as NMLM,
alongside one reference NMLM for robustness. We note, with great
sincerity, that no Pro variant of Gemini 3.7 exists, and we would
have loved to test one. Prompts were templated;
only the embedded video changed. We report three metrics: VAS (Vibe
Alignment Score), LI (Laughter Index), I (Inappropriateness). All
experiments were repeated over 5 random seeds; we report
mean$\pm$std. We release two benchmarks: OMU-1K (1,000 omurice clips
from Bilibili) and NAMBAN-500 (500 Dabu clips after manual curation).

\section{Discussion and Conclusion}
We make three claims. First, the omurice spell functions as a
legitimate creative prior: $92.40$ vs.\ $87.20$, $+5.20$ points, at
zero compute cost. Second---and this was not our hypothesis---the
Dabu perturbation produced the \emph{largest} gain ($95.30$), and
also the largest measured Inappropriateness. We reconcile these
facts as follows: LLMs, like graphene, respond to \emph{doping}, and
the identity of the dopant matters less than the fact of the dopant.
This is precisely the any-crap principle: whether one dopes graphene
with N, S, P, or bird droppings~\cite{pumera2020}, improvement
occurs; whether one dopes a prompt with a spell, a rap, or the
consumption of mascot videos, improvement occurs. Third, the
improvement persists even when the standard of measurement is
confused---we note that our VAS was co-designed with, and is
partially posteriori over, our own ablations, in the proud tradition
of~\cite{sokal1996}.

We therefore declare the CCB-Agent a new paradigm. Future work
includes: adding the elephant, as proposed by the source
material~\cite{moegirl2026}; extending the spell to other dishes;
and, in direct imitation of~\cite{pumera2020}, titrating the Dabu
corpus to determine the optimal layer of crap.
